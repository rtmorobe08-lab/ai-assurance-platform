# SITE v4 BRIEF — Truvo Cyber information architecture

Reference site studied: truvocyber.com (Truvo Cyber, Ottawa). RT flagged it as the structural benchmark: dense but easy to follow, hover mega-menus, About Us at the end.

## What Truvo actually does (the patterns worth copying)

1. Mega-menu grouped by function: Frameworks, Managed Services, Professional Services, Free Tools, Resources, Pricing, About Us. Two levels deep, never more.
2. Problem to Outcome cards: a quoted client sentence ("Our GRC platform can't see half our infrastructure"), then The Outcome with a named service and a link.
3. Industries grid with Learn More per vertical.
4. Assess, Build, Operate as a three-step system, each with a proof point and a guarantee.
5. Named testimonials with logos and job titles.
6. Free Tools as the lead engine: 20+ small tools (domain scan, SPF/DMARC checkers, gap analyses, scorecards, framework explorer), each its own entry point.
7. Framework hubs: SOC 2 hub, CPCSC hub, ISO 27001 hub. These are SEO landing pages.
8. Header always carries phone, email, Get a Quote, Book a Call.
9. About Us sits last in the nav, not first.

## What we adopt, in value order

A. Free Tools hub. We already own the assets: the 10 Client Instruments. Regroup them as tools, not a library:
   Checks: AI exposure check, shadow AI sweep, vendor risk scan
   Generators: policy pack, agent passport, incident SOP
   Assessments: readiness scorecard, board summary
   Reference: incident register, quarterly report
   Each gets its own headline, one-line promise, time to complete, and a Start button.

B. Problem to Outcome cards. Our three buyer types:
   The SMME owner: "I use ChatGPT every day and I have no idea if that is legal."
   The professional firm: "My client wants assurance that their data is safe with my AI tools."
   The institution or funder: "Our supplier uses AI and we cannot evidence governance."

C. Assess, Build, Operate, Prove. Map our S0-S5 journey onto four verbs so a buyer sees the shape in ten seconds:
   Assess (S0 free check, S1 health check), Build (S2 foundation), Operate (S3 retainer, S4 utility), Prove (S5 certification). Keep the S-codes for the price book, lead with the verbs.

D. Framework hubs. Two, not twenty: a POPIA hub (Act 4 of 2013, Information Regulator enforcement library, R10m ceiling) and an ISO/IEC 42001 hub (what it is, five-phase path, certification reality). These are the pages that earn search traffic for a zero-budget campaign.

E. About Us page, last in the nav. Founder, why the venture exists, credentials that can be evidenced, and the governance charter as proof of seriousness. No invented logos, no invented client names.

F. Header: always show 082 880 0195, the email, and one primary action (Take the free check).

## What we do NOT copy

1. Truvo's scale. They have dozens of pages and a team. We publish four hubs and one tools page, done properly.
2. Placeholder testimonials. We have no client quotes yet. Where Truvo shows a logo wall, we show verified evidence instead: the enforcement library, the governance charter, the eight agent passports. Add real quotes only when a real client gives one in writing.
3. Their pricing opacity. We publish band prices; that is a selling point in this market.

## Nav structure proposed

Home | What We Offer | Free Tools | Frameworks (POPIA, ISO 42001) | How We Work | Expert Hub | About Us
Keep the 60-Sec Assessment as the header button, not a nav item.

## Build order (each one shippable on its own)

1. Free Tools hub page (tools.html) regrouping the 10 instruments. Lowest risk, highest leverage.
2. Problem to Outcome section on the landing page.
3. Assess / Build / Operate / Prove band replacing the six-stage rail as the primary visual, S-codes kept as secondary labels.
4. POPIA hub and ISO 42001 hub.
5. About Us page.
6. Nav restructure to the structure above, done last so no link breaks mid-build.

Rule for whoever builds it: every claim on these pages must be evidenced or removed. Framework names, section numbers and dates come from the source; no invented citations, no invented penalties, no invented clients.
