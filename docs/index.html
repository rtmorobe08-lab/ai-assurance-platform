POPIA Is Not Optional Anymore

The Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) applies to every enterprise in South Africa that touches personal information — client records, employee files, supplier data, or anything an AI tool is fed. The Information Regulator is actively enforcing it, and enterprises are required to:

  • Process personal information lawfully, for a defined purpose, and keep it minimal
  • Secure it against breaches — and notify the Regulator when one happens
  • Appoint and register an Information Officer and maintain a PAIA manual
  • Give people the right to object to decisions made solely by automated processing — including AI

Serious offences carry penalties of up to R10 million in fines and/or imprisonment. Every unvetted AI tool, unsecured API key and ungoverned agent in your business is a live POPIA exposure. The longer you wait, the more expensive the reckoning.
