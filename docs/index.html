How We Work

A rigorous, transparent operating methodology designed for skeptical boards, auditors, and regulators.

1. The Six Gates (G0 — G5)

Every engagement is structured around six immutable gates. Nothing moves forward and nothing gets billed without explicit client sign-off at each gate:

G0: Diagnostic
G1: Scope & Audit
G2: Framework
G3: Agent Go-Live
G4: Defense Review
G5: Certification

2. The 13-Week Onboarding Timeline

Foundation executed in weeks 1-6; governed agents live by week 9; active defense enabled; first monthly review and quarterly report delivered on schedule.

3. Communication Rhythm

Weekly status notes during S1-S3; daily 07:00 executive briefs once live; monthly reviews; quarterly service reviews; 24-hour incident acknowledgment and 72-hour formal reporting.

4. Client Prerequisites & Commitments

Requires a designated sponsor who can sign gates, an accountable AI owner, roughly 3 hours at Diagnose, 14-22 hours across Foundation, and 1 hour per month thereafter.

5. Security & Data Handling

Client instruments run entirely client-side. Nothing is transmitted unless explicitly exported. Data Processing Agreements (DPA) available on request.
